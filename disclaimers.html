TULUPEDIA MAKES NO GUARANTEE OF VALIDITY.

Tulupedia is an online open-content parody encyclopedia; that is, a voluntary association of one company attempting to develop a common resource of human knowledge about things named Tulu. Nothing here constitutes professional advice, except the implicit advice to double-check what things are called before naming a company.
